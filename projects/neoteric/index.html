Neoteric

From an idea to a working consultancy, and a 2,50,000-impression campaign.

The problem

Neoteric was a new consultancy with good ideas and little else: no aligned team, no way of running day to day, no brand, and a government PR campaign to deliver.

What I did

At Bizarc Ventures we built the business around the idea. We helped hire and align the team, wrote the standard operating procedures, and automated the routine work so they could focus on clients. We wrote the brand story and designed the identity: logo, colours and imagery.

I designed and ran the government PR campaign with a 45-member team, standard SOPs and compliance checks on every outreach step.

What came of it

A new consultancy went from an idea to a working business, and ran a government campaign that generated 2,50,000+ impressions.

  • A team, written ways of working and a brand of its own: logo, colours, imagery and story.
  • A government PR campaign run by 45 people with standard steps and compliance checks on every outreach step, generating 2,50,000+ impressions.
